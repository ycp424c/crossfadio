# Explicit Music Requests Outrank Selection Pressure

Crossfadio will treat history-derived Selection Pressure as subordinate to the listener's current Explicit Music Request. During autonomous selection, Selection Pressure may suppress recall or lower ranking, but it must not block an explicitly requested Music Entity; unrevoked Explicit Exclusions, objective playback constraints, and queue idempotency still take precedence. This preserves personalization without allowing inferred history to overrule current listener control.
